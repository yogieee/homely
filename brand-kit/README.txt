HOMELY BRAND KIT

01 Logo Mark
  homely-emblem               Full-colour emblem (cream frame, mahogany H). Use on dark or photo backgrounds.
  homely-emblem-reversed      Mahogany frame, cream H. Use on light backgrounds such as ivory or white.
  homely-emblem-one-colour    Single mahogany colour, for stamps, embossing, stickers and one-colour print.
  homely-monogram             The H and leaf on its own, for small spaces such as favicons and app icons.
  SVG files are vectors and scale to any size (signage, print). PNG files are 2000px tall with transparent backgrounds.

02 Logo With Text
  Horizontal, stacked and wordmark-only versions, each in two colourways:
  "for-light-backgrounds" (mahogany text) and "for-dark-backgrounds" (cream text). Transparent PNG.

03 Profile Images (1080 x 1080)
  For Instagram, Facebook, Google Business, WhatsApp and TikTok. The logo sits inside the circle crop.

04 Cover Images
  Facebook 1640x624, X (Twitter) 1500x500, LinkedIn 1584x396, YouTube 2560x1440, Instagram story 1080x1920.
  The logo is kept clear of the areas where platforms place the profile picture.

05 Colours
  Palette sheet and colour codes (HEX and RGB), plus the brand fonts.

06 Pattern
  The Homely damask tile with the H in every medallion. Repeats seamlessly.

Spacing: leave clear space around the logo at least the width of the H on every side.
Do not stretch, recolour outside the palette, or add effects to the logo.
